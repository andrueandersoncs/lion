Kestrel BioLabs Preliminary FY25 Update
Published: 2026-01-15
URL: https://kestrelbio.example/press/fy25-prelim

Kestrel BioLabs announced preliminary unaudited FY25 revenue of $255 million across its clinical laboratory operations.

The Services division closed fiscal 2025 with 410 licensed staff across four regional hubs.

Operational note: Kestrel's clinical-assays line depends on both diagnostic-kits and reagent-vials. Both diagnostic-kits and reagent-vials depend on CryoPack for sterile cold packaging, and CryoPack relies on Helios Glassworks for specialty borosilicate vials.
