Item 5 — Voting Ownership Chain (Solstice Orbital Systems):
- Aegis Space Fund directly holds 15% of the voting shares of Solstice Orbital Systems.
- Aegis Space Fund holds 60% of the voting equity of Vanguard HoldCo.
- Vanguard HoldCo holds 50% of the voting equity of OrbitSPV.
- OrbitSPV directly holds 80% of the voting shares of Solstice Orbital Systems.
