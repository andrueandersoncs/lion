Item 1 — Operations & Reliability Disclosures:
- Following final audit adjustments, FY25 contracted launch backlog was restated to $812 million (revised down from preliminary figures due to deferred milestone recognition).
- Solstice operated 4 active launch pads across its coastal and equatorial sites during FY25.
- CryoTech serves as Solstice's sole primary liquid-methane propellant supplier under a five-year supply contract.

Item 7 — Audited Mission Reliability Constraints (FY25):
- Total audited FY25 orbital launch attempts (s + f) did not exceed 43 missions.
- Successful orbital missions (s) did not exceed 37, and successful missions were at least 21 more than twice the number of anomaly or scrubbed missions (s >= 2f + 21).
- Audited anomaly or scrubbed missions (f) were at least 6 and at most 10.

Note 12 — Commercial Launch Pricing & Volume Rebate Covenant:
- Capacity-adjusted commercial launch slots N ranged between 4.0 and 14.0 slots.
- Contracted per-slot launch price P ranged between $12.0 million and $22.0 million.
- Under the anchor-customer volume-discount rebate schedule, effective price and slot volume satisfy P + 2N <= 36.0 ($M).
