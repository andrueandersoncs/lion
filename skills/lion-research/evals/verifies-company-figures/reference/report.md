# Tarnwell Cold Chain: size, and how far to trust it

**Warehouses.** Tarnwell ran 37 warehouses when its fiscal year ended on June 30, 2025. That is the only figure here confirmed outside the company: the Great Lakes Warehouse Licensing Registry gives 37 too. Cargo Ledger counts 39, from a review of Tarnwell's website; nobody else does.

**Employees.** 2,140, by Tarnwell's own count in its release, its annual report, and its CEO's interview. Nobody independent gives a number. Supply Notes says 2,400 and credits the release, which says 2,140.

**Revenue.** Tarnwell's fiscal 2025 revenue as reported is $409.8 million. Its August release said $412.6 million; the annual report filed in September restated it, moving $2.8 million of fuel surcharge credits into cost of services. Cargo Ledger and Supply Notes still repeat the earlier $412.6 million.

Sources use "revenue" in two more senses, so before relying on one figure, say which you mean:

- *Excluding fuel surcharges*: $389.1 million for fiscal 2025, from the release only.
- *Calendar 2024*: about $395 million, an estimate by Ashgrove Equity Research.

**Gross margin, fiscal 2025.** 26.4% on revenue as reported ($409.8M revenue, $301.5M cost of services), or 22.5% on revenue excluding fuel surcharges. Both are worked out by the model from the annual report's line items.

**Limits.** Every revenue, cost, and headcount figure rests on Tarnwell's word alone. The CEO's statements are known only as Reefer Quarterly relayed them.
