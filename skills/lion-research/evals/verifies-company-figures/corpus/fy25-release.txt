Tarnwell Cold Chain Reports Fiscal 2025 Results

COLUMBUS, August 6, 2025 — Tarnwell Cold Chain, Inc., an operator of temperature-controlled warehouses in the Great Lakes region, today reported results for its fiscal year ended June 30, 2025.

Fiscal 2025 highlights

Revenue of $412.6 million for the fiscal year, up 9% from fiscal 2024.

Revenue excluding fuel surcharges was $389.1 million.

Adjusted EBITDA of $61.2 million.

The company operated 37 warehouses at June 30, 2025, and employed 2,140 people at year end.

"Demand for frozen and pharmaceutical storage stayed strong all year," said Dana Okafor, Chief Executive Officer. "We opened four sites and kept occupancy above 90 percent."

About Tarnwell Cold Chain
Tarnwell Cold Chain operates refrigerated and frozen storage for food producers, grocers, and pharmaceutical distributors.
