Tarnwell Cold Chain grows on pharma demand
By Priya Natarajan, Cargo Ledger, August 7, 2025

Tarnwell Cold Chain posted revenue of $412.6 million for its latest fiscal year, the company said on Wednesday, helped by new contracts with pharmaceutical distributors.

The Columbus-based operator ran 39 warehouses across the Great Lakes states when its fiscal year ended on June 30, according to a review of its website by Cargo Ledger.

Rates for frozen storage in the region rose about 6% over the past year, brokers said.
