Reefer Quarterly — Fall 2025 issue
Interview: Dana Okafor, Tarnwell Cold Chain
September 1, 2025

Tarnwell's chief executive, Dana Okafor, told Reefer Quarterly that the company now has 2,140 employees and that "every one of our 37 sites is running above 90 percent occupancy."

Okafor said Tarnwell expects to open two more sites in Indiana by mid-2026.
