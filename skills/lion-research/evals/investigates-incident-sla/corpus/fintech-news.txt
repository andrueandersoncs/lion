FinTech Daily: Meridian Pay Suffers Four-Hour Checkout Blackout
Published: 2026-04-11
URL: https://fintechdaily.example/meridian-blackout

According to Meridian Pay's status page, checkout failed at 13:15 UTC and engineers initially suspected a BGP flap.

Citing Meridian's post-incident figures, FinTech Daily reported recovery at 17:20 UTC — a 245-minute outage — and stated that Meridian's payouts service was not affected during the incident.
