EnergyWire: Vespera Ownership Confirmed by Nordic Registry
Published: 2026-02-25
URL: https://energywire.example/nordic-vespera

Corporate registry records reviewed independently by EnergyWire confirm that Nordic Energy Holdings holds 35% of Vespera Grid Storage and that Helios Infra Fund holds 60% of Nordic Energy Holdings and 18% of Vespera directly.

EnergyWire also noted that Vespera's Form 10-K caps its total active modular battery sites at 43, per the filing.
