SEC Schedule 13D — Beneficial Ownership of Vespera Grid Storage
Published: 2026-02-20
URL: https://sec.example/vespera/13d-helios

Beneficial voting equity holdings in Vespera Grid Storage:
- Helios Infra Fund directly holds 18% of Vespera Grid Storage voting shares.
- Nordic Energy Holdings directly holds 35% of Vespera Grid Storage voting shares.
- Apex Grid SPV directly holds 24% of Vespera Grid Storage voting shares.

Parent ownership disclosure: Helios Infra Fund holds 60% of the voting equity of Nordic Energy Holdings (contributing 60% of 35% = 21% effective indirect stake in Vespera). No parent ownership of Apex Grid SPV is reported in this filing.
