Research model

Opening the research…

Everything on the other pages is computed from a model of the research, written in a small language called Lion. If you know it, you can ask the model anything here. A query can also add to the model, to see what would follow, and Start over puts the saved model back.

["query", goal] for rows, ["find", value, goal] for a list, ["why", fact] for the reasons. ⌘ or Ctrl + Enter runs.
