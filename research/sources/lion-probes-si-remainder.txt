== program si-remainder-unbounded.json
[
  "query",
  [
    "exists",
    ["?y"],
    [
      "and",
      ["schema/fits?", "?y", "value/integer?"],
      ["number/greaterThanOrEqualTo", "?y", 0],
      ["=", ["number/remainder", "?y", 2], 3]
    ]
  ]
]
== gives

ERROR
  ?y stands for infinitely many values that meet ["and",["schema/fits?","?y","value/integer?"],["number/greaterThanOrEqualTo","?y",0],["=",["number/remainder","?y",2],3]], and ["=",["number/remainder","?y",2],3] waits for its value. The solver cannot reason about number/remainder: it decides sums of numbers times known numbers, comparisons of them, and equality of values. Bound ?y on both sides, or bind it with a relation goal.
