ERROR
  Adding this fact breaks an invariant, so nothing changed.   ["relation", "word", ["letters"]],
  ["relation", "suffix", ["letters"]],
  ["fact", ["word", ["quote", ["a", "b", "c"]]]],
  ["rule", ["suffix", "?xs"], ["word", "?xs"]],
  [
    "rule",
    ["suffix", ["array/tail", "?xs"]],
    ["suffix", "?xs"],
    ["number/greaterThan", ["array/length", "?xs"], 0]
  ],
  ["query", ["suffix", "?xs"]]
]
== gives

ERROR
  Rule ["rule",["suffix",["array/tail","?xs"]],["suffix","?xs"],["number/greaterThan",["array/length","?xs"],0]] computes a new value in its head while recursing, which may never finish. Compute the value in a non-recursive rule, or aggregate it with aggregate/min, aggregate/max, aggregate/shortest, or aggregate/union.
